## Purpose and audience
Supports English teachers and teacher educators through professional development, classroom resources and networking. Bangladesh page describes local online events and curriculum relevance, digital resources for differing resource contexts and teacher language development. Its network section explicitly lists TMTE, A2i, BELTA, ETAB, EETTIPS and TESOL. This confirms BELTA is named on this page, not the scope of any current agreement. Teaching associations are invited to discuss collaboration.

## Main linked resource
https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/
Offers lesson plans, activities, tools, books, research, courses, webinars and teacher educator support. Current homepage lists free courses with certificates and shorter two-hour pathways. Course access, enrolment windows and certificate conditions vary; verify individual course pages before using them in a plan.
Potentially relevant themes include classroom research, reflective professional development, dialogue, climate action, multilingual inclusion and digital competences.
