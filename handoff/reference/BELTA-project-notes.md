# BELTA — discovery notes
Reviewed 13 November 2026. Read-only review; no signup, payment or form submission. No full mobile, accessibility or performance audit.

## Identity
Bangladesh English Language Teachers Association is a nonprofit professional association for English language teaching practitioners. It focuses on teacher development, networking, research and standards of English teaching/learning. Founded 1984; revived 2 September 2003; elected committee followed in January 2005. Historical membership count on history page should not be treated as a verified current count.
Sources: https://www.belta-bd.org/belta-overview and https://www.belta-bd.org/brief-history

## Membership
Teachers at all levels, retired teachers and other ELT professionals are eligible according to the benefits page. Listed life membership fee: BDT 3000. Benefits include event discounts, webinars, publications, SIGs and professional networking. Registration and login routes exist; functionality was not tested through submission.
Source: https://www.belta-bd.org/membership-benefits-fees

## Governance and chapters
2025–2027 committee page lists Professor Ahmed Bashir as President and Dr. Md. Mohib Ullah as General Secretary. Chapters page lists local coordinators and explicitly marks several chapters inactive. Do not infer local inactivity simply from absence of events or equate chapter status with ability to host events.
Sources: https://www.belta-bd.org/executive-committee/2025-2027 and https://www.belta-bd.org/belta-chapters

## Publications ecosystem
https://journal.belta-bd.org/ — official double blind peer reviewed ELT journal; lists Volume 8 as current and still invites papers for June 2024. Publication freshness requires confirmation.
https://stories.belta-bd.org/ — collaborative BELTA and Teachers Helping Teachers (JALT SIG) materials project, with fiction, nonfiction, poetry and reading levels. Visible posts include 2026 material.
Main website also provides newsletter, e-books, reports and downloads.
