## Confirmed content discrepancies / observations
1. Bangladesh page lists Rajaram Paul Chowdhury as Director, Education & Research; National Team lists Vice President.
2. Bangladesh page lists Bedoura Jahan as Director, Finance; National Team lists Director, STEAM Education, and Atia Naz as Director, Finance. Current correct roles need confirmation.
3. Bangladesh page and navigation contain spelling/capitalization issues, including Syhlet, Deputy Diirector, National Competetion.
4. The Bangladesh page navigation exposed an ILPE 2026 URL that returned 404 through the web reader; other pages display ILPE 2027. Recheck in browser before treating as a live global defect because indexed versions differ.
5. NCCA page lists registration deadline 15 October 2026 and national round 30 October 2026. A Register Now announcement is still visible on 13 November 2026. Clarify intended current CTA.
6. Volunteer CTA on Contact resolves back to Contact rather than a distinct volunteer page. Could be intentional; user journey requires clarification.
7. Main menu and footer use different About and ILPE paths and IOELL domains. Audit redirects before changing.
8. Access and exchange counters appeared as zero in text extraction; this could be animation and must not be reported as a confirmed visible bug without visual verification.
9. About page is large enough that the web reader refused it (>4MB); browser successfully loaded it. Performance concern requires measurement, not a confirmed performance finding.
