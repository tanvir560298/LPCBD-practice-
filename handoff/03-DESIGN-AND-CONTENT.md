# Visual and interaction specification

## Direction
Student-first, approachable, credible and contemporary. Navy anchors the interface; warm yellow is an accent. Prefer white/off-white surfaces, readable dark text, consistent borders and modest radii. Establish design tokens for colors, typography, spacing, elevation and component states. Avoid the current heavy mixture of patterned backgrounds and full-width dark sections.

Use the reference image only for direction: split hero, relevant course images and clear teacher portal. Its people, wordmark and data are generated placeholders. No final visual approval has been given.

## Public homepage
Header: recognizable official logo when supplied, concise navigation, accessible dropdowns, teacher portal link and admission CTA. Mobile uses a grouped expandable menu with clear close behavior.
Hero: student benefit, specific supporting sentence and two prioritized actions. Do not make a historical Access event the only first-screen message. No auto-rotating carousel required.
Audience choices: children/teens, students/adults, teacher development. Course cards should use distinct relevant visuals and real metadata rather than repeated generic stationery images.
Bring course discovery ahead of the leadership grid. Detailed leadership belongs on its own page. Highlight program work without confusing scholarship eligibility with paid course admission.
A resource-aware layout should work without video backgrounds, autoplay, unnecessary animation or huge imagery.

## Typography and accessibility
One or two suitable typefaces, comfortable line lengths, clear heading hierarchy and readable mobile body text. Bengali content requires a Bengali-capable font. Use English interface initially with a content structure that can support Bangla; do not add a fake language switch. Translate only when real translations exist.
Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard operation, focus visibility, semantic landmarks, labelled inputs, accessible validation, adequate contrast and touch targets. Status cannot rely on color alone. Respect reduced motion. Verify rather than claim compliance.

## Portal interaction
Teacher home begins with today's assigned sessions and a quick route to attendance. Mobile attendance uses large labelled controls; avoid a cramped horizontal spreadsheet. Search by name or student ID; filter unmarked. Show selected session and saved/unsaved state persistently.
Status colors: present green; absent red; late amber; excused neutral/blue; unmarked gray, always with text. Confirm bulk changes without silently overwriting existing marks. Show summary before final submission.
Display local class times with timezone clarity. Show clear draft and submitted states and explain correction process.

## Useful states
Loading, empty, no assigned classes, search with no matches, invalid login, expired login, unauthorized, network error, save conflict, saving, saved, submission success and failed submission. Browser refresh/navigation must not silently discard dirty edits.

## Assets and copy
Use descriptive image alt text and consistent crop ratios. Optimize image sizes and reserve dimensions. Replace images through a central asset registry. Track asset source/license and whether consent is needed for minors.
Use learner-facing language: course details, related courses and student experiences instead of products, cart and reviews unless genuine e-commerce exists.
Do not add random partner logos, award badges, student counts or testimonials. Legal/privacy copy needs owner review.

## Responsive evidence
Inspect at approximately 360/390px, 768px, 1280px and 1440px. No clipped menu, hidden actions or unintended horizontal page scroll. Test dropdowns with mouse, keyboard and touch. Capture public homepage, course detail and attendance view at desktop and mobile widths.
